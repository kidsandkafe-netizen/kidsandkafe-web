Frequently asked questions

What families ask us most

Can I leave my baby, or do I have to stay?

Whichever you prefer. You can drop them off and go to work — we take care of the rest, and that is the main difference from parenting groups and family spaces, where a parent has to stay in the room. Your little one stays with a specialist carer and you get the morning. And if one day you would rather stay and play together, you can: the passes include two adults.

What are Baby Explorer's hours?

Every morning from 10am to 2pm, with the option to extend from 10am to 4pm including lunch. And if one day you cannot get back in time, the centre is open until 8pm.

Is this a nursery or a preschool?

No. Baby Explorer is the alternative for families who have no nursery place, or who do not want full-time care. It is a play and stimulation space for babies aged 0 to 3 with a specialist carer, in Les Corts, Barcelona.

Is lunch included?

Yes. Lunch is included in the 10am to 4pm option. Please tell us about any allergy or intolerance when you book the place.

What ages does Baby Explorer take?

From 0 to 3 years. The programme is designed to support your baby's development at that stage, with free and sensory play, creative workshops and cognitive stimulation.

Do we have to come every day?

Not at all. You can come for a single day, take a 5 or 10-session pass, or go for the monthly plan if you come every morning. Your choice, and no enrolment fee.

How much does Baby Explorer cost?

A single day costs 25 EUR for the 10am to 2pm slot, and 43 EUR for 10am to 4pm with lunch included. If you come regularly, the monthly mornings plan costs 140 EUR, and there are passes of 5 sessions for 85 EUR and 10 sessions for 130 EUR. There is no enrolment fee. You decide whether to drop them off and go to work or stay and play together: the passes include two adults.

Are you open in summer and during the holidays?

Yes, we are open all 12 months of the year, including the summer months. It is one of the reasons many families choose us over a preschool.
