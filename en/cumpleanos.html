Kids birthday parties in Barcelona with themed decoration included

At Kids & Kafé we've been throwing kids birthday parties in Barcelona since 2018, right in the Les Corts neighbourhood. Every party comes with themed decoration we set up and take down ourselves: balloons, a sweet table and styling to match whatever your little one loves —superheroes, princesses, animals, unicorns or any theme you choose. You just show up and celebrate; we handle the rest.

Our 300 m² room is all yours during the party: a soft play area, a dance floor with lights and sound for the mini disco, and monitors who lead the games so no child is left out. It's the perfect plan for kids aged 0 to 8, with a safe space for the youngest and plenty of activity for the older ones.

Prefer to celebrate at home? We bring the party to you

If home works better for you, we set up the birthday party at your place across Barcelona and nearby towns: we bring decoration, monitors and entertainment, and leave everything tidy afterwards. Choose from our Standard, Premium and VIP packages or room hire, and add a custom workshop as a premium extra to make it even more special. Message us on WhatsApp and we'll confirm your date right away.

Frequently asked

Before you book

How long does the party last?

The Standard, Premium and VIP packages last 3 hours, the same as room hire. The Family Lounge (the small option) is 2 hours. With all of them we give you 30 extra free minutes before the start to set up.

Can we add workshops or activities to the party?

Yes. It's an optional service: we design a bespoke workshop or activity for your little one, tailored to their taste.

Can I bring my own cake?

Ordering the cake from the centre is required. All three packages already include a themed cake for 20 people in the price.

What if more guests come?

The packages include 20 children. From the 20th child onwards it is €7 more per child (on Premium and VIP that price already includes catering) and €3 per additional adult. From 20 children we recommend an extra monitor (€40).

How far in advance should I book?

At least one month ahead. Weekends are the most requested dates and they go fast: when a birthday is booked we close the centre to the public, so each Saturday and Sunday there is room for one celebration only.

What themes do you do?

All of them. Superheroes, princesses, unicorns, dinosaurs, mermaids, astronauts, cars, football… and if your little one wants a theme we've never done before, we'll invent it for them. We make the impossible possible.

Can I see the room before booking?

Of course. You're welcome to come and see the space during our opening hours, at Carrer de Nicaragua 117 (Les Corts). Let us know on WhatsApp at +34 637 319 226 so we can make sure there isn't a private party on at that time.

How much is the deposit and is it refunded if I cancel?

To lock in your date you leave a €100 deposit, which is deducted from the final price. If you need to move the date, 30% is lost and the remaining 70% is kept for the new day. If you cancel definitively, 50% of the amount can be exchanged for other services at the centre: passes, workshops or play hours.
