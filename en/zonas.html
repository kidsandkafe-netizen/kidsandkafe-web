Frequently asked

Before you come to play

Do I need to book to visit the play area?

Booking is required at weekends, because the space may be reserved for a birthday party and closed to the public. From Monday to Friday you can simply walk in, although we always recommend booking. You can book through the Kids & Kafé app, through Google, or by calling +34 637 319 226.

How much does it cost to enter the play area?

The first hour of free play costs 10€ per child and the second hour 7€. Siblings pay 8€ per hour. There is also a 10-hour play pass for 85€, and a 10-hour pass for single-parent families for 75€.

What ages is the play area for?

The play area is designed for children aged 0 to 8. There is a separate Baby Zone for the youngest ones and role-play corners for older children.

What are the opening hours of the play area?

Monday to Friday we open from 9:00 to 20:00. Saturdays and Sundays from 10:00 to 14:00 and from 16:00 to 20:00. We are open all 12 months of the year.

What is inside the play area?

There are 300 m² in total: 200 m² of play space with ten themed corners (Baby Zone, hospital, vet, construction, driving, sensory room, play kitchens, supermarket, library and art room) and 100 m² of dance and psychomotor skills room with a speaker and disco lights.

Where is the Kids & Kafé indoor playground in Barcelona?

At Carrer de Nicaragua 117, in the Les Corts district of Barcelona (08029). Next to the play area there is a Family Lounge with specialty coffee, a kids menu and Wi-Fi for the grown-ups.
