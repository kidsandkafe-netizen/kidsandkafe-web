Frequently asked questions

Everything about the workshops

What kind of kids workshops do you run in Barcelona?

We run six kinds of workshops: art, music, cooking, role play and jobs, cognitive stimulation, and sensory workshops. They all take place at our centre at Carrer de Nicaragua 117, in the Les Corts neighbourhood of Barcelona.

What ages are the workshops for?

The workshops are for children aged 6 months to 7 years. Each workshop states the specific age it is aimed at, because a sensory workshop for babies is not the same as a cooking workshop for a 6-year-old.

When are the workshops held?

Weekday workshops are held in the morning and are designed for mothers and babies. At weekends we also run morning workshops, mainly for the youngest children, aged 6 months to 3 years. The exact dates and times change every month, so it is best to check them in the Kids & Kafé app or by calling +34 637 319 226.

Do you run mother and baby workshops?

Yes. Our weekday morning workshops are mother and baby workshops: the mother or father stays and takes part in the activity together with the baby. They are a way to share time with your baby and to meet other families in the neighbourhood.

How much does a workshop cost?

Workshops start at 25€ per child. The final price depends on the workshop, because each one includes different materials.

How long does a workshop last?

Workshops last approximately 2 to 3 hours, depending on the activity and the age of the children.

Do I need to sign up in advance or can I just turn up?

You need to sign up in advance, because places are limited. You can book through the Kids & Kafé app or by calling +34 637 319 226.

How do I find out which workshops are on this month?

We publish a new workshop programme every month, with different activities. You can see the current schedule in the Kids & Kafé app, on our Instagram (@kidsandkafe), or by messaging us on WhatsApp at +34 637 319 226.
